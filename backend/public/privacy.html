Privacy Policy

Kutunza Gourmet Mobile Application
Effective date: May 3, 2026 · Last updated: May 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Kutunza Foods ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Kutunza Gourmet mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, and delivery address you enter during registration or in your profile.
  • Order information: menu items selected, special instructions, delivery address, and order history.
  • Event booking information: event type, date, time, venue/location, number of guests, and contact details.
  • Payment information: processed securely by our third-party payment provider, Paystack. We do not store your full card details on our servers.
  • Communications: messages you send to our support channels.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Authentication tokens: securely stored on your device (via Expo SecureStore) to keep you signed in.
  • Device information: device model, operating system version, and app version, used for diagnostics and crash reporting.
  • Usage data: basic interaction logs (e.g., orders placed, screens viewed) to improve the service.

1.3 Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not access your camera, microphone, contacts, or photo library.
  • We do not collect precise GPS location data.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers or third-party advertising networks.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the following service providers, strictly to operate the App:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore Database, Cloud Storage) — for sign-in, secure data storage, and account management. See Firebase Privacy.
  • Paystack — to process payments. We do not see or store your full card information. See Paystack Privacy.
  • Railway — hosting provider for our backend API.
  • Expo (EAS) — for app delivery and over-the-air updates. See Expo Privacy.
  • Legal authorities — when required by law, court order, or to protect our rights.

4. Data Security

  • All communication between the App and our servers is encrypted via HTTPS (TLS).
  • Authentication tokens are stored on your device using secure platform storage (Expo SecureStore / Android Keystore).
  • Access to user data on our backend is restricted by Firebase Authentication and server-side role checks.
  • Payments are handled by Paystack (PCI-DSS compliant); we never store full card numbers.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account and order information for as long as your account is active. Order and event records are kept for at least 5 years to comply with tax and accounting laws. You may request deletion of your account at any time (see Section 7).

6. Children's Privacy

Kutunza Gourmet is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information (directly via the Profile screen).
  • Delete your account and associated data.
  • Withdraw consent at any time by uninstalling the App and requesting deletion.
  • Object to certain processing activities.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at the address in Section 10. We will respond within 30 days.

Account Deletion

To delete your account and all associated data, send a request to kutunzafoods@gmail.com from the email address registered to your account. Deletion is permanent and irreversible.

8. International Data Transfers

Our service providers (Firebase, Paystack, Railway, Expo) may process data outside your country of residence. Where required, these providers maintain appropriate safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) to protect your information.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Significant changes will be notified in-app or via email. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

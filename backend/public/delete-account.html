Kutunza Gourmet · Privacy & Data Deletion Requests

You have full control over your personal data in Kutunza Gourmet. You may request deletion of your entire account, or remove only specific pieces of personal data while keeping your account active.

Two options:
  • Delete my account — removes everything (account, profile, history).
  • Delete specific data only — keep your account, but remove selected items (e.g., delivery address, phone number, order history).

What Will Be Deleted (Full Account)

  • Your account profile (name, email, phone number, delivery address)
  • Your saved preferences and settings
  • Your authentication credentials (Firebase Auth record)
  • Your loyalty rewards balance and history
  • Personal identifiers in your order and event-booking history

What We Must Retain

For legal, tax, and accounting compliance, we must retain the following for up to 5 years (anonymized where possible):

  • Transaction records (order totals, dates, payment confirmation IDs)
  • Invoices and receipts required by tax authorities

These retained records will not contain your name, email, phone, or address.
